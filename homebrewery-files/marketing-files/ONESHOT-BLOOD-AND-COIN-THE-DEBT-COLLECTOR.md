# The Debt Collector
## A One-Shot Adventure for Level 1 Characters

**Setting:** Tirvandor Campaign Setting
**Location:** Crossroads, Thaldros frontier
**Duration:** 3-4 hours
**Players:** 3-5 characters, Level 1
**Tone:** Morally gray, consequences matter

---

## Overview

The players are new mercenaries in **Crossroads**, a lawless frontier town where the Iron Guild runs everything. To prove themselves worthy of guild membership, they must complete a simple contract: collect 100 gold pieces from a debtor named Thomas Redhill.

Simple. Except Thomas is a widower with a sick daughter. He borrowed the money to save her life. Now he can't pay.

**What the players do—and how they do it—will define who they are.**

This one-shot introduces the morally complex tone of the **Blood & Coin** campaign. There are no easy answers, no clear heroes, and every choice has consequences.

---

## Adventure Hook

Each character has arrived in Crossroads for their own reasons:
- Exiled from their homeland
- Drowning in debt
- Disillusioned with "heroic" work
- Simply pragmatic about survival

They've heard the Iron Guild offers steady work for those willing to get their hands dirty. All they need to do is complete one contract.

# Part 1: The Contract

## Scene 1: The Copper Dragon Inn

**Read Aloud:**

> *The Copper Dragon's common room reeks of smoke, sweat, and spilled ale. Two dozen mercenaries drink, gamble, and trade war stories. A halfling woman behind the bar watches you with sharp, calculating eyes. In the corner, an old veteran with an eye patch nurses his drink alone.*

**NPCs Present:**

**Mira Copperstone** (halfling innkeeper)
- Runs the inn, sells information
- *"New faces! Welcome to the Dragon. Rooms are 5 silver, meals are 1 silver, information is negotiable."*

**"Lucky" Finn** (retired mercenary, mentor)
- Will approach new arrivals
- *"You lot look fresh. Too clean. Let me tell you how not to die in Crossroads."*

### Finn's Advice

If players engage with Finn:

> *"Three rules. One: Join the Iron Guild—work outside it, nobody protects you. Two: Never break a contract. Ever. Your reputation is everything. Three: Don't trust anyone. Everyone's got an angle."*

**If asked about work:** *"Go see Varak at the Guild Hall. He's got contracts. Prove yourself, you're in."*

---

## Scene 2: The Iron Guild Hall

**Read Aloud:**

> *The Iron Guild Hall dominates the town square—three stories of dark stone, built like a fortress. Inside, mercenaries cluster around a wooden board covered in contracts. Behind a heavy desk sits a dwarf in chainmail, his beard braided with iron rings. He doesn't smile.*

**NPC: Varak Ironfist** (Guild Master)
- Dwarf, 180 years old, gruff, pragmatic
- Values professionalism over morality
- *"New blood. Complete one contract, don't screw it up, and I'll offer membership."*

### The Contract

Varak offers them **The Debt Collection**:

> **CLIENT:** Vareth Goldtooth (loan shark)
> **TARGET:** Thomas Redhill
> **DEBT:** 100 gold pieces, three months overdue
> **PAYMENT:** 75 gold (split among party)
> **TERMS:** Collect by any means necessary. Broken bones acceptable. Death is not—dead men don't pay.
> **BONUS:** 10 gold extra if you "make an example" (public beating)

**If players ask questions:**

*"About the target?"* — "Some laborer. Not your concern. Collect the debt."

*"Why so much?"* — "Interest. That's how loans work."

*"Can we negotiate?"* — "With me? No. With the target? That's your business. Just get the gold."

# Part 2: Finding Thomas

## Scene 3: Investigation

Players must find Thomas Redhill. Several approaches:

### Option A: Ask Around Town

**Gather Information (DC 12):**
- Thomas lives in the Poor Quarter, Willow Street
- Works odd jobs, mostly day labor
- Wife died about a year ago
- Has a young daughter

**DC 15:** "Poor bastard. Borrowed money when his daughter got sick. The medicine worked, but now he's drowning in debt."

### Option B: Ask the Client (Vareth)

**Finding Vareth:** Office behind the Temple of the Coin (gambling hall)

**Vareth Goldtooth** (human loan shark)
- Slick, well-dressed, utterly amoral
- *"Thomas? Willow Street, third house on the left. Blue door. He's been avoiding me for weeks. Make sure he understands that was a mistake."*

**Insight DC 14:** Vareth doesn't care about Thomas—he wants to make an example for other debtors.

### Option C: Go Straight to Willow Street

The Poor Quarter is easy to find. Asking locals for Thomas gets mixed reactions—some protective, some willing to point the way for a few coins.

---

## Scene 4: Thomas's Home

**Read Aloud:**

> *The house on Willow Street is modest—a single room with a loft, blue paint peeling from the door. Smoke rises from the chimney. Through a grimy window, you see a thin man sitting at a table, head in his hands. A child's drawings are pinned to the wall.*

### The Confrontation

**Thomas Redhill** (human laborer, age 34)
- Thin, exhausted, clearly terrified
- Will initially try to flee (Athletics DC 12 to catch)
- If cornered, will beg

**Read Aloud (Thomas Speaking):**

> *"Please! I know why you're here. I'll pay, I swear, I just need more time! My daughter Lily—she was sick, dying. The healers wanted 80 gold for the medicine. Vareth was the only one who'd lend to someone like me."*
>
> *"The medicine worked. Thank the gods, it worked. But then I lost my job. I've been looking for work, I swear. Just give me one more month. I'll pay double. Please... please don't hurt me. Lily's only seven. She can't lose another parent."*

:

### Skill Checks

**Insight DC 10:** Thomas is telling the truth—he's desperate, not deceitful.

**Insight DC 15:** He genuinely intended to pay. This isn't a con.

**Perception DC 12:** Movement in the loft—a young girl watching through the slats.

**Investigation DC 14:** The house is nearly bare. Anything valuable has already been sold.

### If Players Search

- 3 copper pieces in a jar
- A locket with a woman's portrait (his dead wife—he'll beg them not to take it)
- Child's clothes and toys
- Nothing else of value

# Part 3: The Choice

## The Options

The players must decide what to do. Here are the main paths:

---

### Option A: Complete the Contract (Collect by Force)

If players intimidate, threaten, or beat Thomas:

**Thomas's Response:**
> *"All right! All right! Take—take the locket. It's all I have. It was my wife's. Please, it's worth at least 40 gold. I'll get the rest. I'll work double shifts. Just... just don't hurt Lily. She's watching."*

**If players take the locket:** They can return to Vareth with 40gp partial payment. He'll accept it but demand they return for the rest.

**If players beat Thomas:** Lily screams and cries. Neighbors hear. The players complete the contract but:
- Gain reputation as "effective" but "brutal"
- Thomas will never forget
- Lily will never forget

**Contract Completion:** 75 gold, plus 10 bonus if they made it public.

---

### Option B: Pay the Debt Themselves

Players can pay some or all of the debt from their own funds.

**If they pay the full 100gp:**
- Contract complete (they're technically collecting from themselves)
- Thomas is overwhelmed with gratitude
- They've done a good thing—but they're now broke
- Vareth doesn't care where the money came from

**If they pay partial and negotiate:**
- Persuasion DC 14 to get Vareth to accept 50gp now, 50gp in a month
- Gives Thomas time to find work
- Vareth agrees but charges interest (total becomes 60gp in a month)

:

### Option C: Intimidate Vareth

Players could try to pressure the loan shark instead.

**Warning:** This is dangerous. Vareth has connections.

**Intimidation DC 18:** Vareth agrees to extend Thomas's deadline or reduce the debt—but the players make an enemy. He'll remember.

**Failure:** Vareth laughs. *"You think you're the first thugs to threaten me? I have the Syndicate's protection. Touch me and you'll never work in this town again."*

If players attack Vareth, 4 **Thugs** (CR 1/2) arrive in 2 rounds.

---

### Option D: Find Thomas Work

**Investigation DC 14 or asking around:**
- The docks always need laborers (pays 5sp/day)
- A merchant named Cassia Valen needs warehouse help (pays 8sp/day)
- The Guild itself sometimes hires for manual labor

If players find Thomas steady work AND negotiate a payment plan:
- Thomas can pay 5gp/week
- Vareth will accept if Persuasion DC 14
- Contract technically incomplete but debt being paid

---

### Option E: Walk Away (Break Contract)

Players can simply refuse to complete the job.

**Consequences:**
- No payment
- Reputation damaged with Iron Guild
- Varak will not offer membership
- They may be blacklisted from legitimate mercenary work
- Other mercenaries will take the contract—and may be less merciful

**Varak's Response:**
> *"You took a contract and didn't complete it. In this business, your word is everything. You've got no word now. Get out of my hall."*

\page

# Part 4: Resolution

## Returning to Varak

Whatever the players did, they must report to Varak.

### If Contract Completed (Any Method)

> *"Debt collected. Contract complete. Here's your pay."*

**75 gold pieces** (plus 10 bonus if they beat Thomas publicly)

> *"You did the job. That's what matters. Welcome to the Iron Guild. First month's dues waived. Don't make me regret this."*

**Reward:** Iron Guild membership, future contract access

### If Contract Failed/Abandoned

> *"You took a contract you couldn't finish. In Crossroads, reputation is survival. You just damaged yours. Come back when you're ready to be professional."*

**No payment, no membership, damaged reputation.**

---

## Epilogue Options

### Two Weeks Later...

Based on player choices, read the appropriate epilogue:

**If they collected violently:**
> *You see Thomas in the street. He crosses to avoid you. His daughter hides behind his leg, staring with wide, frightened eyes. He's working at the docks—you can tell by his calloused hands. He'll pay the debt. He'll never forget who made him.*

**If they helped Thomas:**
> *Thomas finds you at the Copper Dragon. He's cleaned up—new clothes, steady work. "I wanted to thank you," he says. "Lily asks about the nice people who helped her papa. I'm halfway to paying off Vareth. Because of you, I have hope." He presses a meat pie into your hands. "It's not much. But it's all I have."*

**If they paid the debt:**
> *Thomas named his next dog after one of you. You're not sure how to feel about that. But every time you pass Willow Street, Lily waves from the window. The blue door has fresh paint now. Some debts can't be measured in gold.*

**If they broke the contract:**
> *You hear that another crew took the Thomas contract. They weren't gentle. You see him at the temple of Sera two weeks later, arm in a sling, daughter clutching his good hand. He doesn't know you. Doesn't know you could have been different. Does that matter?*

## Possible Combat

This one-shot may have no combat—it depends on player choices.

**If players attack Vareth:**
4 **Thugs** (CR 1/2 each) — Use SRD stats

**If players get violent with Thomas:**
No combat—Thomas won't fight back. That's the point.

**If players try to rob the Gray Market:**
6 **Bandits** (CR 1/8 each) + 1 **Bandit Captain** (CR 2)

## Moral Framework

This adventure has no "right" answer. The goal is to make players think about:

- **Contracts vs. Conscience:** Is a deal a deal, even when it's unjust?
- **Pragmatism vs. Idealism:** Can you survive in Crossroads with a clean conscience?
- **Consequences:** Every choice ripples outward.

**Do not judge the players.** Whatever they choose, that's who their characters are. The world will respond accordingly.

---

## Hooks for the Full Campaign

If players enjoyed this one-shot, **Blood & Coin** continues their story:

- Vareth becomes a recurring antagonist (or ally)
- Thomas may need help again—or may help them
- The Iron Guild offers increasingly morally complex contracts
- Eventually, they'll choose sides in a brewing civil war

# The Debt Collector

*You're not heroes. You're professionals.*

___

A simple job: collect 100 gold from a debtor. The pay is good. The contract is clear. The target is just some laborer who borrowed more than he could repay.

Except he borrowed it to save his daughter's life.

**The Debt Collector** is a morally gray one-shot that introduces the world of **Blood & Coin**, where everyone has a price and idealism gets you killed.

**What will you do?**

- Complete the contract, whatever it takes
- Find another way to satisfy the debt
- Walk away and face the consequences
- Something the DM hasn't thought of yet

*There are no right answers. Only choices—and consequences.*
