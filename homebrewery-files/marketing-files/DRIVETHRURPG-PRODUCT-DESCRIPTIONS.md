### Full Description (500 words)

**The world is broken. What will you build from the pieces?**

1,247 years ago, the Worldrend tore a single continent in two. Civilizations fell. Millions died. The gods withdrew. And in the chaos, new nations rose—built on secrets, lies, and the bones of the old world.

**TIRVANDOR: THE REALM OF DEEP MAGIC** is a complete campaign setting for D&D 5th Edition, offering everything you need to run years of adventures in a world where nothing is simple and every choice matters.

**WHAT'S INCLUDED (876 PAGES):**

📖 **World Guide (143 pages)** — Complete geography, history, and lore of two continents. From the militaristic empire of Thaldros to the fractious confederation of Aethoria, every region comes alive with detail.

📖 **Player's Handbook (119 pages)** — Custom races, subclasses, and backgrounds tied to the world. New spells, equipment, and character options.

📖 **Dungeon Master's Guide (389 pages)** — 187+ fully detailed NPCs with portraits, stat blocks, motivations, and adventure hooks. Secret histories, faction guides, and everything you need to bring Tirvandor to life.

📖 **Blood & Coin Campaign (37 pages)** — A morally gray mercenary story for levels 1-15. Players start as sellswords taking contracts in a lawless frontier town and rise to become power brokers who shape the fate of nations. No heroes. No villains. Only choices—and consequences.

📖 **Shattered Oaths Campaign (25 pages)** — A heroic campaign for levels 1-15 where players confront Lord Shadows Blackwood, a villain seeking to destroy the kingdoms that murdered his family. His cause is just. His methods are monstrous. What does justice really mean?

📖 **Monster Manual (57 pages)** — Unique creatures native to Tirvandor, from the Void Touched corruption to the Guardian Eyes that watch ancient ruins.

📖 **DM Toolkit (96 pages)** — Quick reference sheets, random tables, timeline summaries, and everything you need at the table.

📖 **Player Guides (10 pages)** — Spoiler-free handouts for both campaigns.

**KEY FEATURES:**

✓ **Moral Complexity** — No pure heroes or irredeemable villains. The rebel leader may burn villages. The tyrant king may genuinely believe he protects his people.

✓ **Living History** — 1,247 years of events shape the present. Every ruin tells a story. Every noble house carries ancient grudges.

✓ **Two Campaign Tones** — Choose gritty mercenary survival or epic heroic adventure—in the same world.

✓ **187+ NPCs** — Every major character has portrait art, full stat blocks, personality notes, secrets, and adventure hooks.

✓ **SRD 5.2 Compatible** — Built entirely on open content. No proprietary material required.

**PERFECT FOR:**

- DMs who want a complete setting without building from scratch
- Tables that enjoy moral complexity and consequences
- Groups who want options—run one campaign or both
- Anyone seeking professional-quality homebrew content
