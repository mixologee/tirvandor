# Introduction

Welcome to **Tirvandor: The Realm of Deep Magic**, a complete campaign setting for Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition. This free primer introduces you to the world, its history, and its conflicts—everything you need to decide if Tirvandor is right for your table.

## What Makes Tirvandor Different?

**Grounded Fantasy**
Magic exists but doesn't solve everything. Politics, economics, and human nature drive conflict as much as dragons and demons.

**Moral Complexity**
No pure heroes or irredeemable villains. The rebel leader seeking justice may burn innocent villages. The tyrant king may genuinely believe he's protecting his people.

**Living History**
1,247 years of recorded history since the Worldrend cataclysm. Every ruin has a story. Every noble house carries ancient grudges.

**Two Complete Campaigns**
The setting includes two full campaigns (levels 1-15 each):
- **Blood & Coin:** Morally gray mercenary story
- **Shattered Oaths:** Heroic campaign against a sympathetic villain

# The Two Continents

## Thaldros (The West)

**Capital:** Kaer Thandros (pop. ~45,000)
**Government:** Hereditary monarchy under House Thandris
**Philosophy:** Order, hierarchy, central authority
**Strengths:** Military power, industry, unified command
**Weaknesses:** Rigid class structure, internal rebellion

Thaldros is a land of dramatic contrasts—snow-capped mountains give way to arid deserts, which transition into temperate woodlands. The Kingdom of Thaldros has ruled for over six centuries, maintaining peace through military strength and political marriages.

But cracks show in the foundation. Noble houses scheme against each other. The frontier territories chafe under distant rule. Rebels called the Red Wolves strike from the forests. And an ancient conspiracy threatens to tear the kingdom apart from within.

### Key Locations

**Kaer Thandros** — The mountain fortress-capital, built into living rock. Seat of King Aldric IV Thandris and the Crown's power.

**Silverpine** — University city famous for its Magical Academy. Center of learning and commerce.

**Crossroads** — Lawless frontier town governed by the Iron Guild mercenary company. Where both campaigns can begin.

**Goldreach** — Wealthy port city controlling western sea trade. Home to the powerful Merchant League.

**The Ashfall Barrens** — Volcanic wasteland where the ancient red dragon Infernalix sleeps beneath the capital.

:

## Aethoria (The East)

**Capital:** None (rotating council)
**Government:** Confederation of city-states
**Philosophy:** Freedom, democracy, individual rights
**Strengths:** Innovation, naval power, magical research
**Weaknesses:** Fractious politics, slow decision-making

Where Thaldros values order, Aethoria celebrates diversity. The eastern continent is a patchwork of independent city-states, each with its own government, customs, and laws. The Aethorian Harmony—a loose alliance—coordinates defense and trade, but consensus is hard-won.

Aethoria is older than Thaldros in some ways, home to elven kingdoms that remember the world before it broke. Yet it also embraces change, welcoming new ideas and new peoples. This openness is both strength and vulnerability.

### Key Locations

**The Starfall Citadel** — Floating fortress of the Aethorian Harmony. Neutral ground for continental diplomacy.

**Wildgrove** — Sacred druidic city where the fey still walk. Heart of the Sylvan Covenant.

**Port Aethermere** — Greatest trading city in the east. Multicultural hub where anything can be bought.

**The Shattered Coast** — Where the continent broke during the Worldrend. Ruins of pre-cataclysm civilizations still emerge from the waves.

# The Worldrend

*Year 0 CR — The Day the World Broke*

No one alive remembers the Worldrend, but everyone lives in its shadow. 1,247 years ago, a single unified continent was torn apart by forces that mortal minds struggle to comprehend.

## What Happened

The official histories speak of hubris—of mages who reached too far, of gods who withdrew their protection, of an artifact called the **World-Splitter** that did exactly what its name suggests.

The truth is darker and more complicated.

Before the Worldrend, five noble houses were entrusted with guarding the World-Splitter's five components. These **Guardian Houses** swore sacred oaths to keep the artifact separated forever. For millennia, they succeeded.

Then something changed. Betrayal. Ambition. Fear. The details are lost to history—or deliberately hidden. What matters is that the World-Splitter was assembled, the ritual was performed, and the world screamed.

## The Aftermath

- **The continent split** — One landmass became two, divided by the Sundering Sea
- **Millions died** — Entire civilizations vanished beneath the waves
- **Magic scarred** — Ley lines fractured, creating zones of wild magic
- **The gods withdrew** — Divine intervention became rare, faith tested
- **Knowledge lost** — Libraries burned, histories forgotten, secrets buried

## The World-Splitter Components

Five artifacts, each tied to a primal force:

| Component | Domain | Guardian House |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Shadow Shard | Darkness | House Blackwood (destroyed) |
| Flame Heart | Fire | House Goldshore (keepers murdered) |
| Void Fragment | Space | House Deepwater (lost to sea) |
| Stone Crown | Earth | Crown of Thaldros (location secret) |
| Storm Eye | Air | Aethorian Harmony (protected) |

If all five were reunited, the World-Splitter could be activated again. The Second Worldrend would make the first look merciful.

*Someone is trying to gather them.*

# The Seven Ascended

The gods of Tirvandor are not distant abstractions—they are forces that shaped history and continue to influence the present. The Seven Ascended rose to divinity during or shortly after the Worldrend, filling the void left by older gods who fell silent.

## The Pantheon

### Thandros, the Forgefather
*God of Creation, Craft, and Civilization*
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Domains:** Forge, Knowledge, Order
- **Symbol:** Hammer striking anvil
- **Worshippers:** Smiths, builders, kings

The patron of Thaldros (the kingdom named for him), Thandros values order, progress, and the works of mortal hands. His temples are workshops where prayer and craft intertwine.

### Aethor, the Windlord
*God of Freedom, Change, and the Sky*
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Domains:** Tempest, Trickery, Travel
- **Symbol:** Four winds spiral
- **Worshippers:** Sailors, rebels, wanderers

Patron of Aethoria, Aethor teaches that change is the only constant. His followers resist tyranny and embrace adventure. His temples have no doors—all are welcome, none are trapped.

### Moira, the Weaver
*Goddess of Fate, Knowledge, and Magic*
- **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Domains:** Arcana, Knowledge, Fate
- **Symbol:** Silver thread through eye of needle
- **Worshippers:** Wizards, scholars, seers

Moira sees all possible futures and guards the secrets of magic itself. Her followers seek knowledge above all—some say at any cost.

:

### Kael, the Shieldbearer
*God of Protection, Justice, and Honor*
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Domains:** War, Protection, Light
- **Symbol:** Shield with sunburst
- **Worshippers:** Paladins, guards, judges

Kael demands that the strong protect the weak. His paladins swear oaths they keep unto death. His judgment is harsh but fair.

### Sera, the Lifegiver
*Goddess of Healing, Nature, and Growth*
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Domains:** Life, Nature, Peace
- **Symbol:** Blooming flower
- **Worshippers:** Healers, farmers, midwives

Sera teaches that all life is sacred and interconnected. Her followers heal without discrimination and tend the wild places.

### Mordain, the Doomkeeper
*God of Death, Memory, and Endings*
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Domains:** Death, Grave, Twilight
- **Symbol:** Hourglass with skull
- **Worshippers:** Morticians, historians, the grieving

Mordain is not evil—he simply tends the boundary between life and death. His followers ensure proper burials, hunt undead, and preserve the memories of the fallen.

### Sylvara, the Shadowdancer
*Goddess of Secrets, Night, and Transformation*
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Domains:** Twilight, Trickery, Night
- **Symbol:** Crescent moon over shadow
- **Worshippers:** Rogues, spies, those with secrets

The most mysterious Ascended, Sylvara protects those who work in darkness—whether for good or ill. Her followers keep secrets, even from each other.

# Major Factions

Power in Tirvandor flows through organizations as much as individuals. These factions shape politics, wage shadow wars, and offer opportunities for adventurers.

## The Iron Council

*Military alliance defending Thaldros*

**Leader:** Lord Commander Gareth Lightbane
**Philosophy:** Honor, duty, protection of the realm
**Headquarters:** Ironhold Fortress
**Strength:** Elite soldiers, veteran commanders, fortress network

The Iron Council coordinates Thaldros's military defense, from border patrols to dragon hunts. Its commanders are bound by oaths of service, and its soldiers are the realm's finest.

**Adventure Hooks:** Border skirmishes, monster hunts, military mysteries

---

## The Merchant League

*Economic powerhouse controlling trade*

**Leader:** Trade Prince Lucian Goldfingers
**Philosophy:** Profit above politics
**Headquarters:** Goldreach
**Strength:** Wealth, information networks, economic leverage

The League cares nothing for borders or ideologies—only commerce. Its agents broker deals between enemies and fund both sides of wars. Critics call them amoral. The League calls itself realistic.

**Adventure Hooks:** Caravan protection, trade negotiations, smuggling operations

---

## The Unbound

*Terrorist cult seeking the Second Worldrend*

**Leader:** Lord Shadows Blackwood
**Philosophy:** Destroy the corrupt kingdoms, vindicate House Blackwood
**Headquarters:** Raven's Keep (hidden)
**Strength:** Fanatical loyalty, ancient secrets, World-Splitter knowledge

The Unbound believe the current order is built on lies and murder. Their leader, the last heir of destroyed House Blackwood, seeks to prove his family's innocence by triggering a new cataclysm. His followers are true believers—and that makes them dangerous.

**Adventure Hooks:** Cult investigations, artifact recovery, confronting sympathetic villains

:

## The Sylvan Covenant

*Protectors of the wild places*

**Leader:** The Circle of Elders
**Philosophy:** Balance between civilization and nature
**Headquarters:** Wildgrove
**Strength:** Druidic magic, fey allies, control of forests

The Covenant watches over Tirvandor's remaining wilderness. They negotiate treaties between cities and forests, hunt those who despoil nature, and guard secrets older than human civilization.

**Adventure Hooks:** Environmental threats, fey politics, ancient ruins

---

## The Crimson Syndicate

*Organized crime network*

**Leader:** The Faceless (identity unknown)
**Philosophy:** Everything has a price
**Headquarters:** Unknown (cells in every major city)
**Strength:** Corruption, assassination, blackmail

The Syndicate's fingers reach into every shadow. They run smuggling operations, protection rackets, and the occasional revolution—whatever pays. Rumor says they've existed since before the Worldrend.

**Adventure Hooks:** Urban intrigue, heists, underworld politics

---

## The Iron Guild

*Professional mercenary company*

**Leader:** Guildmaster Varak Ironfist
**Philosophy:** Contracts are sacred, professionalism above morality
**Headquarters:** Crossroads
**Strength:** Veteran fighters, reputation, neutrality

The Iron Guild takes contracts from anyone who can pay. They don't ask why—they ask how much. This neutrality makes them useful to every faction and trusted by none.

**Adventure Hooks:** Mercenary contracts, moral dilemmas, frontier justice

# The Two Campaigns

Tirvandor includes two complete campaigns, each offering a different experience of the same world.

## Blood & Coin
### A Morally Gray Mercenary Story

**Levels:** 1-15
**Tone:** Gritty, consequences-driven, morally complex
**Starting Location:** Crossroads
**Central Theme:** Everyone has a price—what's yours?

You're not heroes. You're professionals. Sellswords who trade violence for coin in a world where idealism gets you killed.

**Blood & Coin** follows mercenaries who start taking contracts for the Iron Guild and gradually rise to become power brokers who shape the fate of nations. Along the way, they'll:

- Collect debts from desperate widowers
- Guard smuggling shipments
- Choose sides in a brewing civil war
- Uncover conspiracies that reach the throne
- Decide whether to become heroes, villains, or something in between

**Key Features:**
- Reputation system tracking standing with multiple factions
- Morally gray choices with real consequences
- No clear good guys or bad guys
- Rising stakes from street-level to world-shaking
- 37 pages of campaign content

*"The contract said alive. It didn't say unbroken."*

:

## Shattered Oaths
### A Heroic Campaign Against a Sympathetic Villain

**Levels:** 1-15
**Tone:** Epic, emotionally charged, morally nuanced
**Starting Location:** Silverpine
**Central Theme:** What happens when the villain might be right?

Lord Corvus Blackwood—known as **Lord Shadows**—wages war against the kingdom that destroyed his family. At age twelve, he watched his parents executed for treason they didn't commit. Now he commands a fanatical army and seeks the World-Splitter to trigger a second cataclysm.

His cause is just. His methods are monstrous. And the heroes must stop him.

**Shattered Oaths** challenges players to:

- Uncover the truth about House Blackwood's destruction
- Navigate a conspiracy reaching the highest levels of power
- Confront a villain who deserves justice, not death
- Choose between protecting a corrupt kingdom or allowing catastrophe
- Decide what justice really means

**Key Features:**
- Mystery-driven investigation across two continents
- Villain with legitimate grievances
- Multiple endings based on player choices
- Ancient artifacts with world-ending power
- 25 pages of campaign content

*"The kingdoms called my family traitors. They were wrong. And I'm going to prove it—even if I have to burn the world to ash."*
