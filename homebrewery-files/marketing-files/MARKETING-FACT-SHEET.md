## Long Description (250 words)
Welcome to Tirvandor, a world where two continents separated by the Sundering Sea have been locked in ideological conflict for three thousand years. The militaristic Kingdom of Thaldros values order and tradition. The Aethorian Republic champions freedom and progress. And caught between them are adventurers trying to survive.

This complete 5th Edition campaign setting includes everything you need:
