# Tirvandor Discord Server Structure

A complete guide to setting up and managing the official Tirvandor community Discord.

---

## Server Settings

**Server Name:** Tirvandor: The Realm of Deep Magic
**Server Icon:** Tirvandor logo or World-Splitter symbol
**Banner:** Map of both continents
**Verification Level:** Medium (5-minute wait)
**Content Filter:** Scan messages from everyone

## Moderation Guidelines

### Rules Enforcement

**Warning → Timeout → Kick → Ban**

1. **First offense:** Verbal warning
2. **Second offense:** 1-hour timeout
3. **Third offense:** 24-hour timeout
4. **Fourth offense:** Kick with option to return
5. **Fifth offense:** Permanent ban

**Immediate ban for:**
- Slurs, hate speech, discrimination
- NSFW content outside designated channels
- Doxxing or harassment
- Spam bots

### Spoiler Policy

**Blood & Coin and Shattered Oaths channels require spoiler tags for:**
- Major plot reveals
- NPC secrets
- Campaign endings
- Twist revelations

**Format:** `||spoiler text here||`

## Growth Strategy

### Week 1-2: Foundation
- Set up all channels and bots
- Invite close friends/playtesters
- Test all systems
- Create welcome content

### Week 3-4: Soft Launch
- Announce on Reddit (with free content)
- Share in TTRPG Discord servers
- Invite content creators
- Begin regular posting

### Month 2+: Community Building
- Weekly discussion prompts
- Monthly community events (one-shots, art contests)
- Highlight community content
- Feature session recaps

### Engagement Ideas

**Weekly Threads:**
- **Lore Monday:** Deep dive into setting element
- **NPC Wednesday:** Spotlight an NPC
- **Session Friday:** Share your session stories
- **Art Sunday:** Community art showcase

**Monthly Events:**
- Community one-shot (DM runs for members)
- Art/writing contest
- "Ask Me Anything" with creator
- Lore Q&A stream
